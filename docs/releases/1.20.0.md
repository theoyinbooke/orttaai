# Orttaai 1.20.0

Orttaai 1.20.0 makes your Mac the hub for every device you dictate on: Orttaai on Windows and Linux and Orttakey on Android link to it, and it passes their dictations and Words on to iCloud and your iPhone.

## Orttaai Link

- **Link a PC or an Android phone in Settings › Devices.** Click Link a Device: a PC finds the Mac on your Wi-Fi and types the 6-digit code; a phone scans the QR code. Away from home, copy the link for a PC and paste it there.
- **Your Mac passes it all on.** What a linked device sends is saved here and uploaded to iCloud like the Mac's own, so a dictation said on Windows shows up on your iPhone.
- **PCs sync everything both ways**: dictations, Words, snippets, learning suggestions and writing insights. Android phones work like your iPhone: their dictations come in, and Words and your Mac's stats go out.
- **At home or anywhere.** Devices reach the Mac on your Wi-Fi, or by its Tailscale name when the Tailscale app runs on the Mac. Changes made while the Mac is off or away wait on the device and arrive when it's back.
- **Private by design.** Each device trusts only this Mac's key and gets its own key in return. Removing a device in Settings › Devices cuts it off at once. Nothing passes through a server of ours.
- macOS asks once for Local Network access, so devices on your Wi-Fi can find the Mac.

## Where you said it

- **History labels every dictation with its device**, like Claude Code on your ThinkPad, and filters by device. So do Analytics' filters.
- **Settings › Devices shows each device** with its platform, words, dictations and time spoken, and when it last synced.
- Your iPhone shows PCs and Android phones by name and platform, like "ThinkPad · Windows".

## One name per app

- A PC names apps by their program file and the Mac by their name, so Chrome showed up twice. Top Apps, Words by App, writing insights and the summary your iPhone shows now give Google Chrome, Microsoft Word, Visual Studio Code and other common apps one name on every device.

Linking needs Orttaai 0.4 on Windows or Linux, or Orttakey 1.1 on Android. Your iPhone shows the new devices from Orttakey 1.0 (21).
