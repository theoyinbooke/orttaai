# Orttaai 1.19.0

Released November 8, 2026.

Orttaai 1.19.0 brings every Polish engine under one switch, keeps the polish model ready while you talk, syncs your preferences between Macs, and clears out settings that did nothing.

## One switch for Polish

- **Apple Intelligence is a Polish engine.** Pick S1-mini, your local model server (Ollama or LM Studio), or Apple Intelligence under Model › AI Features. Before, Apple Intelligence ran as its own pass with its own switch: it could polish while Polish showed Off, and polished twice with both on. If you used it on its own, it stays your engine with Polish on. Where Apple's model can't run, Polish uses S1-mini and Settings says why.
- **The model you pick is the one that runs.** Model names containing "llama" were quietly swapped for another model in polish, insights and graph summaries. They aren't any more.

## Polish without the wait

- **S1-mini warms up as soon as it's chosen**, at launch, in Settings, from the menu bar or through iCloud sync. The first polish after loading took 656 ms and later ones 75 ms; now your first dictation doesn't pay that.
- **Your local model stays loaded for an hour** instead of five minutes, and starting a recording warms it if it went idle, so it loads while you talk instead of after you stop.

## Your settings on every Mac

- Appearance, Celebrate Milestones, Voice Editing, graph summaries and your other preferences now sync between your Macs through iCloud, and a synced appearance applies at once.
- Settings tied to one Mac stay on that Mac: the polish engine and its models, Decode While I Speak, Tap Right Option (it needs that Mac's Input Monitoring), Launch at Login and setup.

## Cleaner settings

- **Low Latency Mode is gone.** Express covers speed, and the mode barely changed decoding.
- Settings that did nothing were removed, and several descriptions now say exactly what a setting does and where it applies.
- Launch at Login shows the system's real login item and tells you if a change fails.
- Your polish timeout and length choices now stick instead of being reset to old defaults.
