# Orttaai 1.16.0

Released October 28, 2026.

Orttaai 1.16.0 adds **Coach**, in beta: a place to see how you talk, practise one habit at a time, and prove it stuck. It's built on the dictations already on your Mac, and everything it does runs on your Mac. The models run in Ollama or LM Studio, and the voices are made locally.

## Coach (beta)

- **Your voice shape.** Coach counts what you already dictate (fillers, pace, sentence length, repeats, how much Polish changed) and draws a shape for each way you talk: to AI, to people, and on the page. It compares the last 28 days with the 28 before. Apps you switch off are never read.
- **Path and Studio.** 19 skills across four tracks: Prompting AI, Messages to people, Delivery and Explaining. Practise with short reps you speak or type: redo one of your own dictations, a drill, or a 60-second brief. Each rep is scored on counted checks and on yes-or-no questions a local model answers. Your coach writes a short note in one of five personalities.
- **Scenes as conversations.**
  - Five roleplays: brief an AI agent, push back on a client's scope, ask a teammate for a review, give your manager an update, and explain your work to a newcomer.
  - The other person replies out loud in a natural voice of their own, sentence by sentence as the reply is written.
  - Your turn ends when you pause. With headphones on, you can cut in just by talking; otherwise press Space.
- **Proof.** Start a habit and keep dictating as usual. Coach compares 20 dictations before with 20 since, and only calls a habit working when it moved more than your other measures did. **Your month** compares each month with the one before, with a card to share.
- **A judge you can trust.** Before a local model scores anything, it takes Coach's judge check: 52 dictations with plain answers. Only a model that gets both the yes and the no examples right is used.
- **Set up in one go.** The first time you open Coach, a short card picks models for your Mac. It downloads them through Ollama, fetches the voices, checks the judge, and sets everything up. You can change any of it in Cast › Models.

Coach is new, so expect rough edges. It can be switched off in Settings › Coach.

## Also

- **The installer opens in its design every time.** The disk image no longer flashes Finder's plain icon view before its styled window, and its background matches Orttaai's cream.
- **Memory** has moved under Manage in the sidebar.
