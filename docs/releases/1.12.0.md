# Orttaai 1.12.0

Released October 23, 2026.

Orttaai 1.12.0 adds Express, a much faster English dictation engine, and moves every AI feature onto your own Mac.

## Express dictation engine

- **Express** runs Fluid Speech Mini, a compact English speech model, on the Neural Engine. Setup now recommends it on macOS 15 and later; your Whisper model stays selected for other languages and as the fallback.
- On the project's polish test set, Express finished transcription and cleanup in 96 ms at the median, against 725 ms for Whisper Large V3 Turbo.
- **Word Boost** checks the words in your Memory dictionary against the audio, so names and terms come out spelled your way. On recordings of a real voice, Express with Word Boost made 7.9% word errors.
- Numbers come out the way Whisper writes them: `555-0143`, `$2,450`, `March 15th`, `3:30 p.m.`.
- A 10-minute Express dictation finishes in under 2 seconds, and a quiet last word is no longer dropped.
- **Decode while I speak** (off by default) decodes each stretch of speech at your pauses while you are still talking. On long recordings it finished in 100 ms instead of 270 ms at the median, with the same or better accuracy than decoding the whole recording on every test set. It stays off while Word Boost has dictionary words to check.

## AI runs only on your Mac

- Chat, Insights, Memory Graph, Polish and tone of voice now run only through **Ollama** or **LM Studio** (including LM Studio's Bionic app). ChatGPT (Codex) and Grok are no longer supported.
- Cloud models those apps can offer, such as Ollama's cloud models and LM Studio Secure Cloud, are never listed or used.
- If you used ChatGPT or Grok, Orttaai switches to the local server it finds running and picks installed models for each feature, then tells you once in Settings › Model › AI Features.
- When a feature needs a model you haven't installed, Orttaai asks first, then has Ollama or LM Studio download it. Semantic memory can use `nomic-embed-text` the same way.

## Learning and Chat AI

- **Learn from My Edits**: when you fix a word right after dictating, including in a chat box before you send the message, the fix appears in Memory › Suggestions. Nothing reaches your dictionary until you accept it, password fields are never read, and only the word pair is kept.
- **Chat AI** streams its replies, renders Markdown (headings, lists, code and tables), and has a Codex-style composer, a floating chat history with search, rename and delete, and quick-start prompts. It is the place to ask about your own dictation.

## Redesigned screens

- New first-run **Setup** with animated steps, a fixed-size window and smooth step changes.
- Redesigned **Overview**, **Analytics** and **Graph Insights** screens, with faster scrolling. The Insights panel shows a live summary of your last 30 days.
- The Performance card names the engine dictation actually runs on.
- Apple's Writing Tools button no longer appears inside Orttaai's text fields.

## Smaller fixes

- The clipboard is restored as soon as a paste lands instead of after a fixed wait.
- Whisper live dictation no longer copies the whole recording on every poll, which cut memory use on long recordings.
- History and other open pages update as soon as a dictation finishes.

Download the DMG, open it, and drag Orttaai into Applications. Existing direct-download installations can use **Check for Updates**. Homebrew installations update with `brew upgrade --cask theoyinbooke/orttaai/orttaai`.

Validation: 796 tests passed, 9 skipped, and no failures. Live integration tests and UI automation were excluded.
