# Orttaai 1.14.0

Released October 25, 2026.

Orttaai 1.14.0 polishes dictation with a built-in model, turns spoken numbering into real lists and long dictations into paragraphs, learns from your edits in Electron and Chrome-based apps, remembers your microphone, and keeps a large iCloud sync from freezing the app.

## Polish built in

- Text Polish can now run **S1-mini by Superwhisper**, a small model trained only to clean up dictation, inside Orttaai on your Mac's GPU, with no Ollama or LM Studio server to keep running. It removes fillers, resolves self-corrections and punctuates without rewriting what you said.
- It works a paragraph at a time, so long dictations are polished too instead of being skipped. Anything that can't be polished safely or in time goes in unpolished, and dictionary words always come through unchanged.
- S1-mini is a one-time 484 MB download in Model › AI Features › Text Polish, and it is now the default **Polish Engine**. If you used Polish with Ollama or LM Studio, download S1-mini there, or set Polish Engine back to the local LLM server to keep your model.

## Formatting

- Say **"First, … Second, …"** or **"Firstly: … Secondly: …"** and the items become a numbered list, the same as "number one, number two". It can mix the two ("First, … Number two, …"). A single "First, let me check…" or "the first time" stays as you said it.
- A long last item no longer swallows the rest of the dictation: it keeps its opening sentences and what follows becomes its own paragraph after the list.
- **Automatic Paragraphs**: a long dictation that came out as one block is split into paragraphs at sentence ends, preferring the places where you move on ("Also", "Another thing", "Finally"). Never in Terminal and other command-line apps, and not when you said "new line" or "new paragraph" yourself. It's on by default, with a switch in Settings › Text.
- Restarts like "we don't we don't need them" are tidied to one copy. Phrases such as "over and over" and "step by step" keep their meaning.

## Learn from My Edits in more apps

- Claude, Slack, T3 Code, ChatGPT and Chrome now let Orttaai read the box you dictated into, so your fixes there are noticed. Before, most checks in these apps showed "This app didn't let Orttaai read the box".
- Names you capitalize mid-sentence (ola → Ola) and fixes that sound alike are learned; contractions and capitals that start a sentence are ignored.
- A fix to a word Orttaai already knows shows as "Fixed a word Orttaai already knows" instead of "Learned".

## Microphone and dictation quality

- Your chosen microphone is remembered by an ID that survives disconnecting, so AirPods and other Bluetooth mics are found again after they reconnect. A chosen mic that isn't connected records from System Default instead of failing. If you picked a mic before, choose it once more in Settings › Audio.
- History notes which microphone each dictation came from, and keeps what the speech model heard before cleanup when it differs from the final text.
- Express no longer swaps an ordinary phrase for a dictionary word spelled nothing like it (for example "Mac mini" for a name in your dictionary).
- Express no longer turns up the quiet pause after your last word, which could add stray words at the end of a dictation, especially on AirPods.

## iCloud Sync

- Syncing and setting up sync run off the main thread, so a first sync or merge with thousands of items no longer freezes the window.
- Setting up sync downloads your iCloud data once instead of three times.
- The two setup choices are readable: the recommended Merge Both Devices is a tinted card with light text instead of gray text on amber.

## Overview and About

- Top Apps shows your top four apps.
- The Insight button has a new icon.
- The About page is redesigned around the Orttaai mark, with creator and open-source links together and acknowledgments in a compact grid. Automatically download updates uses Orttaai's own switch.
