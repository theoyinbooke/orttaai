# Orttaai 1.18.1

Released November 1, 2026.

Orttaai 1.18.1 fixes two ways a dictation could end early or go missing. Hands-free recordings now listen for your voice instead of loudness. A Bluetooth headset mic that resets mid-recording no longer loses what you said.

## Hands-free that hears you

- **Auto-stop listens for a voice.** When Stop After Silence is on, Orttaai now uses a small voice detector (Silero VAD) to decide when you've finished. A soft voice, a quiet wireless mic or a louder headset no longer reads as silence partway through a sentence. On real recordings it heard 95–96% of speech at every mic level, against as little as 28% before. It's also the only check that notices you've stopped when there's fan noise in the room.
- **A one-time 1 MB download.** The detector downloads into your model folder the first time a hands-free recording can use it. It's pinned to one version and checked before use. Until it's there, or if it ever fails, Orttaai falls back to the loudness check, which now also hears quieter mics.

## Headset mics that keep going

- **Recording recovers.** macOS stops recording when a Bluetooth headset switches to call mode, a mic is unplugged or the default input changes. Orttaai now starts the same mic again and keeps everything already captured, or carries on with the Mac's default mic if that one is gone.
- **Long dictations aren't thrown away.** If some audio still goes missing but most of it arrived, the text is inserted and the pill says some audio was lost. Before, you saw "Audio was dropped" and lost the whole dictation.
- **Settings › Audio** now says when the mic in use is a Bluetooth headset, and suggests your Mac's microphone for the clearest dictation.
