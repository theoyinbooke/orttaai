# Orttaai 1.18.0

Released October 28, 2026.

Orttaai 1.18.0 adds a one-key way to dictate. Tap Right Option to start talking, and tap it again to put the text at your cursor. Quick taps of the dictation shortcut also work every time now.

## Tap Right Option to talk

- **Settings › Dictation › Tap Right Option to Dictate** turns it on. It's off until you choose it.
- **Tap once to start a hands-free recording, and tap again to insert the text.** Either key can stop a recording the other one started.
- **Typing is untouched.** ⌥ characters such as é and ™, ⌥ shortcuts, ⌥-clicks and holding the key never start a recording.
- **It needs Input Monitoring.** Settings shows an Allow button until macOS grants it, and the key starts working as soon as it's allowed, without a restart. The choice stays on this Mac and isn't synced.
- **One limit:** while a password field or another app's secure keyboard entry is active, macOS hides key presses, so the key doesn't respond. Your dictation shortcut still works there.

## Every tap counts

- **A tap of the shortcut now lasts up to half a second.** A press between about a third and half a second used to stop the recording at once and drop it as too short, with no message.
- **A soft sound** now plays when a tap switches a recording to hands-free, so you know you can let go.
