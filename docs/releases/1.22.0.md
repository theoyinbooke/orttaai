# Orttaai 1.22.0

Released November 19, 2026.

Orttaai 1.22.0 adds Express Pro: a second Express speech model that makes a few fewer mistakes and dictates in 25 languages, just as fast.

## Express Pro

- **Choose Express or Express Pro** in Settings › Model › Speech. Express Pro runs Fluid Speech Pro by FluidVoice, the most accurate open speech model under a billion parameters. It's a one-time 622 MB download (Express stays 241 MB) and dictates as fast as Express.
- **25 languages.** Express Pro dictates English and Bulgarian, Croatian, Czech, Danish, Dutch, Estonian, Finnish, French, German, Greek, Hungarian, Italian, Latvian, Lithuanian, Maltese, Polish, Portuguese, Romanian, Russian, Slovak, Slovenian, Spanish, Swedish and Ukrainian. Choose your language in Settings › Dictation, which now lists them all. With Auto-detect, or a language Express doesn't cover, dictation uses your Whisper model as before.
- **Switching never interrupts dictation.** While Express Pro downloads, Express keeps dictating; if the download or the first load fails, Express stays on with the model you had. Picking Express again calls off a download in progress.
- Word Boost and number formatting are English-only, so they run on English dictation and leave other languages as the model wrote them.
- Changing the dictation language loads the right Express model instead of falling back to Whisper until the next dictation.

## Fixes

- If the dictionary, snippets or Polish step fails, your dictation is now inserted as you said it instead of being lost with "Couldn't transcribe".
- Setup and the Remove Express Models button follow the model you chose.
