# Orttaai 1.23.0

Released November 25, 2026.

Orttaai 1.23.0 learns how your words sound when you fix them, and makes voice editing more dependable.

## Fixes learn how your words sound

- **Correct a word once and Orttaai learns its sound.** Say a name the speech model keeps writing as an everyday word (a product called "Eimailar" heard as "email", say), fix it, and Orttaai learns how your word sounds. Next time, saying your word gives your word, and saying the everyday word leaves it alone. Before, a fix became a text rule that rewrote every "email" you ever said.
- It works with Express in English while Word Boost is on. Learned words show as themselves in Memory and in the toast.

## Voice editing

- **Edits keep your line breaks.** An edit no longer joins the lines it touched.
- **A model that's still loading gets time.** When the edit model has to load first, Orttaai waits for it instead of giving up.
- **The selection is checked again** after waiting for the app, so the edit applies to what's actually selected, with one History row per edit.
- **Everyday phrases stay dictation.** "Put it on the list" and "text me" are written as you said them, not treated as instructions.
