# Orttaai 1.15.2

Released October 27, 2026.

Orttaai 1.15.2 keeps hands-free recording going on a quiet microphone, pastes into Codex faster, no longer freezes on a clipboard item from your iPhone, and uses less memory and CPU.

## Hands-free

- On a quiet microphone, hands-free stopped itself within a minute or two while you were still talking: most of a sentence counted as silence. What counts as speech now follows your microphone, from the room's own level and your loudest moments, so a quiet mic keeps recording and a loud room stops after the same silence as before.

## Pasting

- **Codex**: the clipboard goes back as soon as your text shows up in Codex, within 150 ms, instead of after a fixed 400 ms. A Codex dictation now goes in in about 0.2 s instead of about 0.6 s, and is still pasted only once.
- When the clipboard held something copied on your iPhone or iPad, saving it before the paste could freeze Orttaai for up to 20 seconds while the Mac fetched it from the device. The save now gives up after a second: the paste goes ahead, and your dictation stays on the clipboard in place of that item. Voice edit commands that copy your selection do the same.

## Lighter and smoother

- **S1-mini** keeps a smaller context loaded: with S1-mini Polish loaded, Orttaai uses about 430 MB instead of 655 MB, with the same results. A dictation of more than about 580 words gets a larger context of its own, freed when it's done, and takes about 0.3 to 0.6 s longer.
- The floating pill no longer keeps animating after it hides. After a dictation or a hover it used the CPU for as long as Orttaai ran; now it uses none.
- While you record, the pill draws its level bars itself instead of being rebuilt for every new level, using about a third less CPU. Its waves hold still under Reduce Motion; the bars still follow your voice.
- **Home** and **Analytics** work out their numbers in the background. Opening them, switching range or device, and dictating with Home open no longer freeze the window, which took up to 0.6 s per click on a large history. Analytics fades in once its numbers are ready.
- The trend chart switches to new data straight away instead of animating it, which cost a lot of work on every switch; the rest of the dashboard still animates.
