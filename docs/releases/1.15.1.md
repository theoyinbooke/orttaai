# Orttaai 1.15.1

Released October 25, 2026.

Orttaai 1.15.1 fixes a crash on quit and dictations losing their delivery details, makes Polish's lists dependable, and breaks Analytics down by device now that your iPhone and iPad dictate too.

## Analytics, by device

- Once more than one kind of device has dictated, **Showing: All, This Mac, iPhone, iPad** picks which devices every card counts.
- A **By Device** card shows each device's words and dictations in the range.
- Phone dictations count as **Orttakey on iPhone** (or iPad) in Words by App, not "Unknown".
- Each dictation now records the time zone it was said in, and Time of Day and Weekly Rhythm chart it in that zone.
- Performance is always this Mac's speed, and says so once other devices dictate.
- Dictations from before devices were tracked count as this Mac's, unless another Mac shares the archive.

## Lists and paragraphs

- A list ends where its paragraph does: "new paragraph" or "new line" after the last item, and the text after it, stay as you said them.
- Polish's answer is no longer undone afterwards: lists and paragraphs it lays out come through as they are.
- Two lists in one dictation are both laid out, each with its own introduction.
- **Lettered lists**: say "letter a, … letter b, …", or "a) … b) …", for A., B. lines. Letters said inside a numbered item nest under it.
- "I bought 2. Then I bought 3." stays a sentence, ", and third," continues a list, and lists go past ten.

## Fixes

- Quitting or updating Orttaai after Polish had run ended in a "quit unexpectedly" report. Polish's model is now freed before Orttaai quits.
- Since 1.15.0, each dictation's delivery details (how the text was inserted, the final timing) were saved to the wrong place and lost. They're saved with the dictation again; the ones already lost can't be recovered.
- When iCloud asks Orttaai to slow down, as it can during a first upload of a large history, Settings now says "iCloud is busy, retrying shortly" instead of showing an error, and an error from Sync Now clears once sync gets through.
- The same suggestion made on two Macs shows once, and accepting or rejecting it decides both.
