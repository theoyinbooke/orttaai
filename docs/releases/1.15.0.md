# Orttaai 1.15.0

Released October 25, 2026.

Orttaai 1.15.0 rebuilds iCloud Sync so your Mac and Orttakey on iPhone and iPad share one archive: what you dictate on your phone shows up in History and in your stats, your dictionary goes both ways, and sync sends only what changed, encrypted.

## Your iPhone and iPad dictations, on your Mac

- With **Orttakey**, Orttaai's voice keyboard for iPhone and iPad, and iCloud Sync on in both, every dictation you make on the phone arrives in Orttaai's History.
- They count everywhere: Overview, streaks, milestones, insights, the Memory Graph and semantic search. Older ones synced in are indexed too.
- Your **dictionary** is shared both ways, including "say this, write that" pairs, which Orttakey now applies as well.
- Your phone keeps only its own dictations. It shows your total across every device from your Mac's summary.

## History

- Each dictation shows the device it was said on. When you have dictations from more than one kind of device, a filter shows **All, This Mac, iPhone, iPad** or **Other**.
- A dictation from the phone shows **Orttakey on iPhone** (or iPad) where the app would be, since iOS doesn't tell a keyboard which app it's typing in.
- The transcript view keeps your line breaks, and shows **You said** when the speech model heard something different from the final text.
- With sync on, deleting a dictation deletes it on every device, and the confirmation says so.

## iCloud Sync, rebuilt

- Sync sends only what changed and fetches only what's new, instead of downloading and re-uploading everything every time.
- Your history is updated in place: the Memory Graph, embeddings, edit checks and badges are no longer rebuilt after each sync.
- Everything you dictated and wrote is stored in iCloud in **encrypted** fields.
- Deleted dictations are really deleted from iCloud, and stay deleted on every device. A dictionary word you delete comes back only if you add it again.
- Settings sync key by key, so changing one setting on one Mac never undoes another setting changed on another Mac. Chats sync one conversation at a time, so a long chat history no longer runs into iCloud's size limit; deleting a chat deletes it everywhere.
- **Settings › iCloud Sync** has a switch, the status, the devices sharing your archive, and clear choices if your iCloud copy was removed, your storage is full, or another iCloud account signs in.

## Updating

- On first launch, Orttaai backs up its database, then prepares it for the new sync. With sync on, it then uploads your archive once in the new format, which can take a while for a large history.
- Macs still on Orttaai 1.14 or earlier keep syncing with each other, and their dictations reach this archive, but they won't see new changes until they're updated. Settings shows when one was last seen.
