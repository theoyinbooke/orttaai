# Orttaai 1.15.3

Released October 28, 2026.

Orttaai 1.15.3 makes dictation much faster: with Express, the text is usually transcribed before you let go of the key, and pastes are quicker. A paste no longer ends up on the clipboard when another window briefly takes the front, and Performance on Home shows numbers you can judge changes by.

## Faster dictation

- **Express transcribes in the pause before you let go.** Most people stop talking a moment before releasing the key; Orttaai now starts transcribing then, Word Boost included, so there's usually nothing left to transcribe when you let go. If you start talking again, it waits for your next pause. Measured on real dictations: transcription after release went from about 250 ms to 0 ms; a typical dictation into Claude now takes 130–370 ms from key release to text, down from 650–750 ms. In a noisy room, where Orttaai can't be sure nothing was said after the pause, it transcribes at release as before.
- **Quicker pastes.** Milestones no longer reads your whole history while a dictation is being pasted (it held each paste up by about 80 ms), and a 30 ms pause before pasting now only happens when the app actually has to come to the front. Pastes into Claude take about 45 ms, into Codex about 180 ms.

## Pastes that wait for their app

- When a system alert or another app's window took the front while your dictation was being processed, Orttaai couldn't bring your app back (macOS doesn't allow it) and left the text on the clipboard. It now waits up to 8 seconds for your app to come back, then pastes. It never pastes into whatever happens to be in front, and checks again for a password field first.

## Performance you can read

- **Performance** on Home shows the typical (median) times of your last 50 dictations instead of a 7-day average, so a change shows the same day and one slow dictation doesn't skew it. **Rules & polish** is now measured rather than worked out from the other two. Analytics' Performance section uses the same dictations and adds a Rules & polish column.
- Each paste now records how long each of its steps took in the system log (numbers only), for diagnosing slow pastes.
