# Orttaai 1.17.0

Released October 28, 2026.

Orttaai 1.17.0 adds a light look and gives most of the app a calmer, rounder design. A slim rail of icons stands in for the sidebar when you collapse it. Coach on your Mac can now show its progress on your iPhone.

## Light and dark

- **A light look.** Cream pages, white cards and a deeper gold, so gold buttons and gold text stay easy to read. The sidebar stays dark in both looks.
- **Settings › General › Appearance** chooses Match Mac (the default), Light or Dark. If your Mac is set to light, Orttaai opens in the light look after this update. The recording pill and milestone toasts stay dark wherever they float.
- **Coach** follows the same setting. Its rail and the weekly and monthly share cards stay dark.
- **The title bar** takes the colour of the page beneath it, so it never shows as a band in either look.

## A fresh look

- **Rounder cards** across Analytics, Settings, Model, Memory, Graph, History, Milestones, About and Coach. Home keeps its layout and cards exactly as they were.
- **Analytics** leads with a warm card for the range's word count.
- **Settings, Model and About** read as one centred column, so each control stays close to its name on a wide window.

## A slim sidebar

- **Collapse the sidebar** and a slim column of section icons takes its place on every page, with a button to bring the sidebar back. Orttaai remembers your choice.

## Coach on your iPhone

- **Your Mac's Coach progress** now travels with the summary it already shares with Orttakey: your habits and how they're going, and where each practice track stands. It's names and numbers only, never anything you said. The "On your Mac" card appears in Orttakey's Coach tab after its next update.
