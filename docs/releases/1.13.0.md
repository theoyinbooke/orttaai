# Orttaai 1.13.0

Released October 24, 2026.

Orttaai 1.13.0 adds Milestones, shows Learn from My Edits at work, and makes updates visible from the moment a new version is out.

## Milestones

- A new **Milestones** page with 22 badges in bronze, silver and gold: words dictated, streaks, speed, typing time saved, dictation counts, and a few specials such as Three Apps, Marathon, Wordsmith and Night Owl. It shows your next goal and how far you are from it.
- Earn a badge and a card appears in the corner, without taking focus or showing anything you dictated. The next time you open Orttaai's window, a celebration pages through everything new. **Celebrate Milestones** in Settings › General turns both off; badges are still earned.
- **Share a card** for any badge you've earned, or for your numbers so far. Each kind of milestone has its own designs, all about that milestone: how tall your words would stand next to the Eiffel Tower, a calendar of a streak, your pace racing handwriting and typing, a stopwatch for your longest dictation, and more. Three sizes (4:5, square and 9:16), saved, copied or sent through the share menu.
- Badges are stored on your Mac, so deleting history never takes one back. Cards show numbers only, never anything you dictated.

## Learn from My Edits, visible

- **Memory › Suggestions** lists what Orttaai found after each recent dictation (a word learned, an edit, left as dictated, or a box it couldn't read), with the app and the time but never the text.
- A **Learned a word** card appears when a fix becomes a suggestion; Review opens Memory › Suggestions, and Memory shows a count in the sidebar.
- Dictations are found however the app stored spaces and line breaks, and a paste that shows up late is read again, so more fixes are noticed.
- Each suggestion says whether it came from your edit or from your history. AI Suggestions no longer save labels like "Misheard term:", and keep a word pair only when the heard word was really said and sounds like its replacement.

## Updates you can see

- Orttaai checks for a new version every 3 hours, and again shortly after launch, when you open its window and when your Mac wakes, if the last check is over an hour old.
- A new version shows at the bottom left of the sidebar. One click downloads it, with its progress on a ring, then installs and restarts. With automatic downloads on, it downloads straight away and waits at **Restart to update**; it also installs when you quit.
- After an update, a short **What's New** card shows the release's highlights.
- Updating from 1.12.0 still goes through the previous update window; the new button takes over from 1.13.0 on.

## Chat AI

- Dictated or typed text in the chat box is now the same size as the rest of the composer. The box grows with your draft up to eight lines, then scrolls, so the last line is never cut off.
- The conversation scrolls on under the composer, and the selected chat in the history panel loses its amber bar.
